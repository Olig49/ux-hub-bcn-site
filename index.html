About us

“We hope to meet you soon, and to hear about your experience and thoughts.” UX Hub Barcelona

We gather to inspire and entertain one another

UX Hub Barcelona is a group for people who want to collaborate with others and contribute to ideas on user experience and design. We meet for insightful sessions in a relaxed atmosphere, to create a genuine community connection.

What is a hub? A hub is a regular gathering of people under the roof of an inspiring host, held to grow what everyone knows through conversation.
