Text tiers, hairlines, and the two accessibility corrections shipped over the brand's raw values.

Text

#323232

Headings, body copy, primary UI text.

Aa

fg-2 — secondary text

#6a6a6a

AA fix: the brand's raw value #777777 measures 4.48:1 on white and fails WCAG AA for normal text. Shipped override is #6a6a6a, which passes.

Aa

fg-3 — muted text

#B4B4B4

Placeholder text, disabled state, least-important meta. Not for body-length copy.

Aa

Lines

line — hairline divider

rgba(0,0,0,0.10)

Card borders, section dividers, table rules. Cards use this or --uxh-shadow-md — never both.

line-strong

rgba(0,0,0,0.30)

Borders around color swatches, donate amount chips.

On dark — amber substitution

Yolk on Teal Night measures only 3.7:1 — fails AA. Use --uxh-amber-on-dark (#F7C77E) instead for any text or icon that needs the "accent" color on a dark surface (e.g. the tier-card price on the Teal Night pricing tier).
