Cards

One family, four shapes — sponsor, offer, team, partner. The one hard rule that applies to all of them: a card carries a hairline border or --uxh-shadow-md, never both.

✓ border only

1px solid --uxh-line

✓ shadow only

--uxh-shadow-md

✕ never both

border + shadow = too heavy

The four shapes

sponsor

White · 1px border · radius 20 · padding 28px · gap 12px.

offer

Radius 24 · padding 32px · min-height 380px · hover lift -4px / 240ms.

team

Warm White · 1px border · radius 20 · padding 14px. Social links 44px min.

partner

On dark · rgba white 6% fill · radius 20 · padding 22px. Two-column ≤620px.

Radii differ by shape (20 vs 24) — see radii.html for the full scale and which component uses which step.
