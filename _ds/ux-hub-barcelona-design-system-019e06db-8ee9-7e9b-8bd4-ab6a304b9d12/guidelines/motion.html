Foundations · Motion

Motion

Calm and conversational, never bouncy. One easing curve, six durations tuned to what's moving. Hover the demos below — they honour prefers-reduced-motion.

Easing

TokenValueUse
--uxh-easecubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)Every transition — a smooth ease-out, nothing linear or bouncy.

Durations

TokenmsUse
--uxh-dur-press80mstransform: scale(0.98) on :active, every button.
--uxh-dur-hover160msColour + background changes — button fills, link colour.
--uxh-dur-move200msSmall positional nudges — trailing arrow icon, translateX(3px).
--uxh-dur-layout240msCard lift on hover, modal card entrance.
--uxh-dur-overlay220msModal / drawer overlay fade.
--uxh-dur-entry400msReveal-on-scroll (opt-in, JS + motion only).

Press states never use --uxh-ease as anything but an ease-out; press is a quick 80ms scale(0.98) only. Page transitions (not shown here) are a soft fade + 8px upward translate — no bounce, no dramatic motion. Any auto-playing motion (marquee, reveal-on-scroll) must stop entirely under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
