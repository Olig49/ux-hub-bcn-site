Forms

MailingForm and DonateForm. Markup below is copied verbatim from UX Hub Barcelona.html; both are homepage-only, no equivalent on sponsors.html.

MailingForm — .mail-form

Soft-beige card, a white pill row for the actual input. Source: UX Hub Barcelona.html:429-437.

Thanks. We’ve opened a new tab, check your inbox to confirm your subscription.

By subscribing you agree to our privacy policy. Unsubscribing is one click. We use it for events, nothing else.

The real form posts to Brevo (`sibforms.com`) — the action URL above is redacted to a placeholder in this catalog so the button can't submit real signups from a documentation page; every class and layout attribute is otherwise verbatim. The role="status" acknowledgement (.mail-ack) is normally hidden until the inline submit handler reveals it (UX Hub Barcelona.html:572-576) — shown here in its default hidden state.

DonateForm — .donate-card

Warm-white card on the dark Donate panel. Source: UX Hub Barcelona.html:396-415, shown here on its native dark surface.

Choose an amount

You’ll be taken to Stripe for secure one-off or recurring payment.

Amount chips and frequency pills are populated/kept in sync by inline JS (UX Hub Barcelona.html:644-716), which also maps the selection to one of six pre-built Stripe Payment Links (three amounts × two frequencies) or a fallback any-amount link for custom values. Hover the info icon (or focus it) to see the fine-print tooltip driven by data-tip + ::after (site.css:425-431).
